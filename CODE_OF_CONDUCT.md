# Code of conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate exposure of private data, and publication of real credentials or account material are not accepted. Technical disagreement is welcome when it stays focused on evidence and the work.

Repository maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors or users.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact methods on the GitHub profile. Do not include unrelated personal or credential data.
